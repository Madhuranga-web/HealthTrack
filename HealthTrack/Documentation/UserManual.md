# User manual

## Client

Register with full name, email and password; public registration creates a Client. Sign in, then use the left navigation. `Health profile` edits goals; `Progress` records height and weight and calculates BMI. `Workouts` lists trainer assigned exercises and accepts completed exercise, duration and burned calorie logs. `Diet` separates recommended targets from food and calorie values you actually enter. `Appointments` books only assigned trainers at UTC times, displays status and allows cancellation. `Notifications` marks messages as read. `Account` updates the display name.

## Trainer

Sign in with an administrator-created account. `Clients` shows assigned clients only; open a client to review real weight, BMI, workout and meal history and send feedback. `Workout plans` and `Diet plans` create, edit and delete plans and add or remove exercises or meals. A workout plan with log history cannot be deleted. `Appointments` approves or rejects pending requests, reschedules a current booking, or cancels it. `Reports` totals activity from currently assigned clients.

## Administrator

Sign in with the controlled local seed account. `Users` searches accounts, creates Client or Trainer accounts, and activates or deactivates them. The admin cannot deactivate their own account. `Assignments` creates and removes trainer/client links. `Appointments` gives a system overview. `Reports` shows engagement counts. `Settings` updates the message displayed on client and trainer dashboards. The dashboard displays real database activity by month.

## Privacy and notices

Only assigned trainers can access client progress. A client can view and modify only their own health data. In-app notifications appear for appointment requests and status changes, new plans and feedback. Scheduled reminders are not implemented.
